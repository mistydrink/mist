Open Review

Free chess game review that runs Stockfish 17 right in your browser. Paste a PGN or import your recent games from Chess.com or Lichess, and every move gets graded from Brilliant to Blunder, with accuracy scores, an evaluation graph and the engine's best move drawn on the board. No server and no paywall, and your games never leave the page.

  • Practice mode turns your mistakes into puzzles
  • Insights across all your saved reviews
  • Share links that carry the whole review in the URL
  • Works offline and installs as an app

Site Starter

A website builder for local businesses that don't have a website yet. Owners snap together blocks like hours, a call button, a price list and a map, watch the site build live, and download one file that works on every phone. There's a free plan and a Premium demo, and everything stays in the owner's own browser.

  • 10 blocks, with an automatic "Open now" from the hours
  • A running receipt that adds up the site as you build it
  • Printable QR code flyer for the shop window
  • Privacy-first: no AI, no tracking, delete everything in one click
